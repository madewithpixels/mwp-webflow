The pixel heart — glyph composition

The mark is a single 9×7 grid of 63 pixel cells (<div class="logo"> with 63 empty <div> children). Three :nth-child() CSS rules colour specific cells so that the three glyphs M · W · P (madewithpixels) interlock into a heart — one accent colour each. Cells not targeted stay blank.

On the live site the pixels are 7px (10px at ≥1024px) with a 1px gap, and each colour group animates in from grey (rotate-grey-pink / -cyan / -yellow) staggered by 50ms. The M sits at columns 1–4, the W nests in at columns 3–7 (one row down), and the P anchors columns 6–9.

Assembled mark

The three glyphs placed on the shared 9-column grid — this is the exact cell layout from the site's :nth-child() rules, rendered here at the base Logo tokens (20px cell, 2px gap, 3px corner).
